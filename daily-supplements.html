Our Supplements

Daily Word for a strong, healthy spirit.

Many people supplement their diet with vitamins to give their body what it may not normally consume, but still needs. We should treat our spirit the same way. If we are not careful, necessary areas of our spiritual life can be neglected.

These Daily Faith Supplements are designed to be read, spoken and meditated on regularly so your faith stays strong, your mind stays renewed and the Word stays at the center of your life.

How to use it

Read it. Say it. Believe it.

Take a few minutes each day. Read the Scriptures slowly, speak them out loud and let the Word shape the way you think, pray and live.

Every Day

Daily Word

Build confidence in God, identity in Christ and a life rooted in the Word.

Foundation

1 John 5:14–15 — This is the confidence we have in Him: when we ask according to His will, He hears us.

1 John 5:4

Our faith is the victory that overcomes the world.

1 John 4:17

As He is, so are we in this world.

1 John 4:4

Greater is He who is in you than he who is in the world.

2 Peter 1:3–4

His divine power has given us all things that pertain to life and godliness.

Matthew 4:4

We live by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God.

Psalm 1:2–3

Meditate in His Word day and night and be like a tree planted by rivers of water.

Jeremiah 29:11

God’s thoughts toward you are thoughts of peace, future and hope.

Jeremiah 33:3

Call to Him and He will answer and show you great and mighty things.

Deuteronomy 7:6–9

You are His chosen people, His special treasure, loved by a faithful covenant-keeping God.

2 Corinthians 5:17

If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away.

Monday

Words

Faith speaks. Use your words to agree with God and His Word.

Foundation

2 Corinthians 4:13 — “I believed and therefore I spoke.” We also believe and therefore speak.

James 3:3–5

The tongue is small, but it directs and influences far more than its size suggests.

Revelation 12:11

They overcame by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony.

Matthew 12:33–35

Out of the abundance of the heart, the mouth speaks.

Isaiah 55:11

God’s Word does not return void; it accomplishes what He sends it to do.

Proverbs 18:21

Death and life are in the power of the tongue.

Psalm 45:1

Let your tongue become the pen of a skillful writer.

Mark 11:23

Believe in your heart and speak to the mountain in faith.

Jeremiah 1:9–10

God puts His words in your mouth and gives those words assignment and authority.

Psalm 34:1

Bless the Lord at all times; let His praise continually be in your mouth.

Tuesday

Peace

Let the peace of Christ settle your heart, mind and decisions.

Foundation

John 14:27 — “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you.” Let not your heart be troubled or afraid.

John 14:1–2

Do not let your heart be troubled. Believe in God and believe in Jesus.

John 16:33

In Christ we have peace. He has overcome the world.

Philippians 4:6–7

Pray about everything and let the peace of God guard your heart and mind.

Isaiah 26:3

He keeps in perfect peace the one whose mind is stayed on Him.

Isaiah 55:11–12

His Word prospers, and you go out with joy and are led forth with peace.

Isaiah 9:6

Jesus is Wonderful, Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father and Prince of Peace.

Isaiah 48:18

God’s peace is pictured like a river and His righteousness like the waves of the sea.

Ephesians 2:14

Christ Himself is our peace.

Colossians 3:15

Let the peace of God rule in your heart, and be thankful.

1 Peter 5:7

Cast all your care upon Him, because He cares for you.

Wednesday

Strength

Receive strength from God for your inner man and every assignment in front of you.

Foundation

Philippians 4:13 — I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.

Ephesians 3:16

Be strengthened with might through His Spirit in the inner man.

Colossians 1:9–11

Be strengthened with all might according to His glorious power.

Psalm 18:1–2

The Lord is your rock, fortress, deliverer, strength, shield and stronghold.

Psalm 18:32–33

God arms you with strength and makes your way secure.

Psalm 27:1

The Lord is the strength of your life; you do not have to fear.

Psalm 46:1

God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

Isaiah 41:10

Fear not. God is with you; He strengthens, helps and upholds you.

Isaiah 50:7

The Lord God helps you; set your face like flint and refuse shame.

Isaiah 40:29–31

Those who wait on the Lord renew their strength; they run and do not grow weary.

Thursday

Prosperity

Prosperity begins with God’s Word, His wisdom, His blessing and His sufficiency in every area.

Foundation

3 John 1:2 — Beloved, I pray that you may prosper in all things and be in health, just as your soul prospers.

Deuteronomy 8:7–9

God brings His people into a good land of provision and abundance.

Deuteronomy 8:18

Remember the Lord your God; it is He who gives you power to get wealth.

Deuteronomy 28:2–13

God’s covenant blessing reaches your life, work, household, provision and influence.

Joshua 1:8

Meditate in the Word day and night; observe it and make your way prosperous with good success.

Psalm 23:1–6

The Lord is your Shepherd; He leads, restores, provides and causes goodness and mercy to follow you.

Psalm 34:9–10

Those who seek the Lord shall not lack any good thing.

Psalm 37:4

Delight yourself in the Lord and He will give you the desires of your heart.

Psalm 112:2–3

The generation of the upright is blessed; wealth and riches are in his house.

Proverbs 10:22

The blessing of the Lord makes rich, and He adds no sorrow with it.

Proverbs 22:4

Humility and the fear of the Lord bring riches, honor and life.

Isaiah 1:19

If you are willing and obedient, you shall eat the good of the land.

Isaiah 48:17

The Lord teaches you to profit and leads you in the way you should go.

Malachi 3:10

Honor God in giving and trust His promise of provision and overflowing blessing.

Luke 6:38

Give, and it will be given to you — good measure, pressed down, shaken together and running over.

John 10:10

Jesus came that you may have life, and have it more abundantly.

Romans 8:31–32

If God is for us, who can be against us? He freely gives us all things with Christ.

2 Corinthians 9:8–10

God makes grace abound so you have sufficiency and can abound to every good work.

Ephesians 1:3

In Christ, God has blessed us with every spiritual blessing.

Philippians 4:19

God supplies all your need according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus.

Matthew 6:33

Seek first the Kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.

Friday

Authority

Know who you are in Christ, the power of His name and the authority He has entrusted to believers.

Foundation

Luke 10:19 — Jesus gives authority over all the power of the enemy, and nothing shall by any means hurt you.

Matthew 10:1

Jesus gave His disciples power over unclean spirits and authority to heal sickness and disease.

Matthew 18:18

What you bind and loose on earth carries spiritual authority in agreement with heaven.

Matthew 28:18–20

All authority belongs to Jesus, and He sends us to make disciples of all nations.

Mark 11:23

Speak to the mountain in faith without doubting in your heart.

Mark 16:15–18

Go into all the world and preach the Gospel; signs follow those who believe.

Luke 9:1–2

Jesus gave power and authority over demons and disease and sent His disciples to preach the Kingdom.

Luke 10:2–3

The harvest is great; pray for laborers and go where He sends you.

John 1:12

Those who receive Him are given the right to become children of God.

Acts 1:8

You receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you, and you become His witness.

Galatians 2:20

Christ lives in you; live by faith in the Son of God.

Revelation 5:10

He has made us kings and priests to our God, and we shall reign on the earth.

Romans 5:17

Those who receive abundance of grace and the gift of righteousness reign in life through Jesus Christ.

Ephesians 2:6

God has raised us up together and made us sit together in heavenly places in Christ Jesus.

Romans 8:11

The Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you and gives life to your mortal body.

Genesis 1:26

Humanity was created in God’s image and given dominion under His authority.

Speak the Word with faith. Authority is not about striving or volume. It flows from union with Christ, agreement with His Word and confidence in what He has already accomplished.
