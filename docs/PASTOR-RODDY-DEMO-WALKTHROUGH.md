## Executive talking points

One connected system and one People foundation. Outreach connects planning, team logistics, registration, event day and follow-up. Leadership sees reports without unrestricted operational permissions. Native Giving is being validated for a deliberate future cutover. Forms, provider-neutral routing and payment recovery are reusable Kingdom Propel capabilities.

GiveHub remains the live giving provider today. Kingdom Propel native Giving is the replacement platform being validated before deliberate cutover.

“NMI integration is built. Actual sandbox-provider acceptance is waiting on the sandbox account credentials.”

## Not live

Native production card processing; NMI or Authorize.Net production processing; provider-proven NMI multi-MID; GiveHub cutover; live recurring charges; real saved-card tokenization; live text-to-give or bulk SMS; QuickBooks integration; complete Serving or Worship Planning.
