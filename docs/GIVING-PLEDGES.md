# Giving pledges and recurring foundation

Pledges are commitments, not charges or ledger gifts. `giving_pledges` scopes reviewed canonical Person/Household, fund, exact target/currency, date range, status, source/campaign and notes. Weekly, biweekly, monthly, custom and unscheduled commitments can have explicit expected installments in `giving_pledge_schedule`. GIV-1 stores the reviewed schedule rather than inventing installment amounts or automatically debiting donors. Status supports active/completed/cancelled/paused/written-off/closed with optimistic revision checks.

`giving_pledge_allocations` connects actual posted gifts to commitments with immutable actor/date/amount/request identity. Donor or reviewed household, fund, currency and active pledge must match. One gift may be partly allocated, but total allocation cannot exceed remaining canonical gift principal. Pledge overage is retained as received > target; expected schedule, received, remaining/overage and actual ledger are distinct. Eight genuinely overlapping allocation requests cannot overspend one gift. Corrections/refunds affecting allocated principal fail closed pending separately reviewed remediation; no allocations are silently deleted.

Recurring instructions are separate from pledges and can contain multiple fund lines, each with a frozen merchant/gateway/MID route. Frequency supports weekly/biweekly/monthly/twice-monthly/custom; start/end, next due, status, failure/provider schedule references and revision are represented. Browser creation is draft-only. Pause/cancel/draft changes are audited; a browser cannot activate a charge. Provider-account and vault capability verification remains necessary, especially cross-MID portability.

Provider customer/payment references live in `private.giving_payment_profiles`, not report projections or raw client tables. Registration is service-only and idempotent to gateway/provider references; rebinding a profile to another donor is denied. No raw card/CVV/bank information is stored. A saved method's display descriptor can be exposed to the separately reviewed donor; references cannot.

Service claim of an eligible due instruction inserts a unique held run at `(instruction_id,scheduled_at)`. Multiple workers yield one claim; no execution, advancing schedule, retry charge or live provider subscription is performed. Future provider activation must define custom/twice-monthly semantics, retry/reconciliation, disabled/paused behavior and whether a vaulted method can serve every route. See [provider gates](GIVING-PROVIDER-ADAPTERS.md) and [ledger](GIVING-LEDGER.md).
