## Exact fictional contract

`campaign-provenance-contract.mjs` accepts compact JSON bytes. Exact schemas, UUIDs, positive integer revisions and canonical UTC instants reject unknown top-level fields, duplicate JSON keys, altered byte encoding and ambiguous dates. The case binds a UUID/version, exact organization/campaign/source/registration/Person/account/task/binding/requester/reviewer/link/registrant, the five canonical revisions, a metadata snapshot digest, policy ID, method and the explicit purpose `canonical-outreach-task-association`. The method `fictional-witness-v1` is a synthetic test marker, **not an approved verification method**. No supplied role, hash or `verified:true` can replace the signed evidence contract.

The test owner supplies three distinct ephemeral Ed25519 public roots before evaluating a proposal: fictional policy/designation authority, fictional source witness and fictional independent reviewer. These keys have no production registration or authority. Separate signed receipts for source, independent Person/account identity, adult status and exact-purpose consent bind original fictional bytes, the entire case, policy and reviewer designation. The signed policy explicitly accepts one method and finite window for each claim; receipt methods must match, observations must fall within acceptance and receipt validity cannot exceed it. Only the four `fictional-<claim>-witness-v1` markers are supported; signing a policy naming an email-matching or other real method cannot approve it. Hashes identify exact bytes; signatures check the fixture's byte chain. Neither proves the truth of a real identity claim. Minor, unknown, self-declared-adult, guardian-assumed and contact-matched substitutions fail. No minor contact or guardian authority is inferred.

Policy and designation carry exact organization/campaign scope and finite validity. A distinct authorizer cannot be the requester, target account or reviewer. The reviewer cannot be the requester or target account. The canonical read-only snapshot now checks the exact authorizer's current confirmed, non-anonymous, not-deleted and not-banned account metadata, matching the existing reviewer/account checks. These account facts do not prove identity or confer authorizer authority: the injected fictional authority root and current signed designation remain the test-only authority source. The previous fixture could accept a completely absent authorizer; a native reproduction established that gap before this correction. All validity ends before the existing finite binding expiry. Evidence retention and audit retention are explicit finite policy dates; the fixture's arbitrary minute-scale test windows are not product retention decisions. Approval cannot precede evidence observation, and reviewer designation must cover each approval/withdrawal event's actual time. Receipt validity, each accepted method and the current designation are rechecked at evaluation time; withdrawal or expiry cannot be cured by a valid historical signature.

The fictional approval/withdrawal journal binds exact case/version, receipts, reviewer, policy dates and predecessor hashes. Withdrawal retains the original approval and ends the case; it is not reactivation or destructive deletion. A separately captured `trustedFixtureHeadBytes` is required at evaluator construction. The proposal cannot replace it. That capture binds the exact policy and designation hashes as well as case, event count and head; its capture time must include the latest event and cannot be in the future. Its finite current-until date cannot exceed policy validity (which already ends before evidence/audit retention). No arbitrary freshness interval is invented. Missing/expired anchor, fork, reordered/replayed events, truncated pre-withdrawal history or changed case with the same version fail. The current-head capture is a fixture trust assumption; a real independently maintained current-head/custody system does not exist. The evaluator cannot discover a new withdrawal when a purported custodian supplies an old still-unexpired head: independent current-head retrieval/revocation remains a real-use gate. There is no persisted audit journal or actual retention/deletion worker here. PR105 already preserves canonical binding withdrawal history; it remains unchanged.

Every output is frozen and permanently has `canLink=false`, `canWrite=false`, `canContact=false`, `realEvidenceVerified=false`. Even `fixture-contract-pass` is only a synthetic structural/cryptographic pass. No output includes a binding insert payload, evidence bytes, target identifiers or reusable authority token. There is no production mode, activate flag, setter or transport. Current canonical metadata cannot override any of these permanent denials.
