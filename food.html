Why we do it

Because people matter to God.

Food outreach is one simple way Champion Life can serve our neighbors, care for families and demonstrate the love of Jesus in a practical way.

01

Serve with dignity

We want every family who comes through our doors to be treated with kindness, honor and respect.

02

Meet practical needs

When resources are available, we want them moving into the hands of families who can use them.

03

Build real connection

If you want prayer, encouragement or a church family, Champion Life is here for you beyond distribution day.
