Online Giving Terms

Voluntary Donations

All gifts are voluntary and support the mission, ministries, outreach and operations of Champion Life Church. Donations are generally final and non-refundable.

Secure Processing

Online gifts are processed securely through GiveHub. Champion Life Church does not store complete card or banking information.

Receipts

A confirmation and receipt will be provided after a successful contribution. Consult a tax advisor regarding deductibility.

Recurring Giving

Recurring gifts are charged according to the schedule authorized by the donor and may be managed through the GiveHub account portal.

Errors

Contact giving@championlifefwb.com promptly if you believe a donation was processed in error.

Acceptance

By submitting a donation, you confirm that you are authorized to use the selected payment method and agree to these terms.
