Privacy Policy

Champion Life Church is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly.

Information We Collect

We may collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, mailing addresses, form submissions, prayer requests, event registrations, volunteer applications and giving-related contact information. Complete credit card and banking information is processed by GiveHub and is not stored by Champion Life Church.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to process donations, provide receipts, respond to requests, register participants, communicate ministry updates and maintain church administrative records.

Sharing & Security

Champion Life does not sell, rent or trade personal information. Data may be shared with trusted service providers such as GiveHub and Jotform when necessary to provide requested services or when required by law.

Children's Privacy

Information about children is collected only when voluntarily provided by a parent or legal guardian for ministry participation or registration.

Contact

Questions may be sent to office@championlifefwb.com.

Refund Policy

All gifts and charitable contributions are voluntary and generally final and non-refundable, except where required by law or in the case of a verified processing error.
