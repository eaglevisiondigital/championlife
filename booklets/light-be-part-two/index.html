Sunday Sermon • Part Two • Highlights

Revelation Knowledge
& Illumination

Pastor Roddy Shaffer  •  Champion Life Church, Fort Walton Beach

Introduction — Highlights

The Light Got In

“God, who said, ‘Let light shine out of darkness,’ made his light shine in our hearts to give us the light of the knowledge of God’s glory displayed in the face of Christ.”

2 Corinthians 4:6 • NIV
  • Big Idea: the same God who spoke light over creation has shone that light in our hearts.
  • Recap Part One — “Light, be!” over a dark, formless creation (Genesis 1:3, NKJV).
  • Part One: light over a world — Part Two: light in a person.
  • Illumination: not just lights on around you — lights on in you.
  • Eyes of your understanding enlightened — know your inheritance, the hope, and the power already yours in Christ (Ephesians 1:17–18, NKJV).
  • Set the expectation: somebody’s light is coming on today.
  • Illustration: lights coming on room by room in a house — one room has stayed dim too long, and today its light comes on.
Point One — Highlights

The Light of the Knowledge of the Glory of God

“But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the excellence of the power may be of God and not of us.”

2 Corinthians 4:7 • NKJV
  • Big Idea: the enemy blinds minds to the gospel, but God commands light — and He put that treasure in you.
  • “It’s OK if you go to church — just don’t let the light come on to the gospel.” The gospel is veiled to those who are perishing; minds blinded by the god of this age (2 Corinthians 4:3–4, NKJV).
  • Believers can be blinded too — to righteousness, healing, authority, and deliverance.
  • God’s answer is the same as Genesis: He commands light to shine in our hearts (2 Corinthians 4:6, NKJV).
  • Revelation knowledge: not figuring God out — God revealing Himself; you see a face, not a theory.
  • Illustration: a clay pot carrying treasure — plain on the outside, glory on the inside.
Point Two — Highlights

The First Light Is the Kingdom

“Most assuredly, I say to you, unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.”

John 3:3 • NKJV
  • Big Idea: you must be born again to see the kingdom — and you have already been delivered and transferred into it.
  • “The first light you should get is on the kingdom of God.” Before you can walk in a kingdom, you have to see it; before you can see it, you must be born into it (John 3:5, 7, NKJV).
  • Religion gives information — only the new birth gives illumination.
  • He has qualified you to share the inheritance of the saints in the light (Colossians 1:12, NKJV).
  • He has delivered us from the power of darkness and conveyed us into the kingdom of the Son of His love (Colossians 1:13, NKJV).
  • The gospel is past tense: already delivered, already transferred, already redeemed and forgiven (Colossians 1:14, NKJV).
  • Illustration: a change of address — you’ve already been moved; stop living as if you still live in darkness.
Point Three — Highlights

You Are Light — Live Like It

“For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Live as children of light.”

Ephesians 5:8 • NIV
  • Big Idea: you are light now — identity comes first, then you live like it in your deeds and in your love.
  • “You were once darkness, and dark deeds came easy” — they matched who you were.
  • But NOW you are light in the Lord — not becoming light, you are light.
  • Identity before behavior: God makes people light, then says live like it.
  • Put aside the deeds of darkness and put on the armor of light (Romans 13:12, NIV) — you don’t drive out darkness by focusing on darkness; you put on light.
  • The test that the light is on: love — anyone who hates a brother or sister is still in darkness (1 John 2:9, NIV).
  • Illustration: getting dressed for the day — put aside the clothes of the night and put on the armor of light.
Conclusion — Highlights

Let the Light Come On

  • Big Idea: name the room, open the door — let the light come on in every area.
  • In your understanding of the kingdom? (Colossians 1:13, NKJV) In an area where the enemy has blinded your mind? (2 Corinthians 4:4, NKJV)
  • In a deed of darkness to put aside and armor of light to put on? (Romans 13:12, NIV) In a relationship where love must prove you are in the light? (1 John 2:9, NIV)
  • Remember who you are: once darkness, now light in the Lord (Ephesians 5:8, NIV).
  • Illustration: opening every door in the house — no locked, dark room left.
LIGHT BE!

Over my mind, light be. Over my family, light be. Over my health, over my future, over Champion Life Church, light be. I am light in the Lord, and I will live as a child of light.

May you be overtaken by His blessing today. Be blessed.
