About Champion Life

Helping people live from who they are in Christ.

Champion Life exists to raise people in Christ, teach them to reign in life and equip them to reach their world.

Meet Pastors Roddy & Danielle Shaffer

Pastor Roddy gave his life to Christ in 1995. His journey has included coaching, youth ministry, Bible college teaching, national business leadership, conferences and pastoral ministry. His passion is helping believers see who they are and what they have in Christ.

Pastor Danielle is a registered nurse, Rhema graduate, gifted Bible teacher and compassionate co-pastor with a deep heart for children, families and people.

Our vision

Raise • Reign • Reach

Raise

Raise people up in Christ through the Word, prayer, discipleship and authentic community.

Reign

Equip believers to reign in life through Jesus Christ and walk in faith, victory and purpose.

Reach

Take the Gospel beyond the church walls into our community, region and world.
